OpenTennis for iPhone / iPad

Add the OpenTennis source to your sideloading app once, then install it and get every update from there. It's signed with your own certificate, so it installs only on your devices.

Add to Feather Add to AltStore Add to SideStore

Nothing happens when you tap? Open this page in Safari on the iPhone with the app installed, or copy the URL and add it under Sources in the app.

After installing

  1. Open the Files app → On My iPhone → OpenTennis (it appears after the first start).
  2. Put your own Hot Shots Tennis (USA, SCUS-97610) ISO there. No game data is included.
  3. Start OpenTennis. Touch controls or a controller.

Or just download the unsigned .ipa and sign it yourself. Back to opentennis.dev

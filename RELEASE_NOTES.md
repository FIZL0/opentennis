- Save and Quit from the pause menu, then pick the match back up from Continue on the main menu (the browser version too).
- After a match: Rematch, New Match (picks kept) or back to the menu.
- Online matches can be resumed after a player drops or the host saves, with everyone back in their seats.
- Phones no longer lock the screen during a match.

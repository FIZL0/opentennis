<!-- What's new since the last release, for players, in plain English: one "- " line per change. tools/release.sh puts this in the release and empties it. -->
- Rushed and early-prepared swings now play at the original's speed, and doubles computer players read their partner's racket at the right moment, so they position themselves like the original in more rallies.
- Doubles computer players now start their run to the ball on the frame they swing, as in the original, instead of a frame late.
- Doubles computer players read their partner's stance correctly after a change of stance or a run, so they position themselves like the original in more rallies.
- Doubles matches now start with clouds in the sky like the original, and every match's opening weather and random events line up with the original's from the first point.
- A ball that hits a player now bounces off them, and ends the point, on the same frame as in the original.
- A player who dives now stays down as long as in the original before getting up, and gets up turning the right way.
- A ball struck the moment after it bounces out now stays in play, as in the original.

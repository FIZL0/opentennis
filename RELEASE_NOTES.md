<!-- What's new since the last release, for players, in plain English: one "- " line per change. tools/release.sh puts this in the release and empties it. -->
- The Continue screen's saved-match cards match the rest of the menus: bigger, with the score on the pause menu's score board and every player's face and name, and no longer cut off the court name or when the match was saved, at any screen shape.
- iPhone and Android: tapping the screen over and over while the game opens no longer leaves the on-screen controls dead. A button or stick stuck on a finger the phone lost track of frees itself the next time you touch it.
- iPhone and Android: the on-screen controls appear only after you tap the title screen to start, and that tap has to land away from the screen's edges and corners, so a finger holding the phone doesn't start the game.
- Playing in a phone's browser: the on-screen controls likewise appear only after you tap the title screen to start.

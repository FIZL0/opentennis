- Settings: the Base Disc list no longer jumps to the next row when you press down twice with the arrow keys or WASD.
- Settings: adding another release's disc is now Gameplay → Add Disc, and Voice Language says how to get more languages while some are missing.
- Changing Voice Language is heard straight away, even mid-match, instead of from the next match.
- The tennis ball is lit evenly and brightly as in the original, instead of looking dull and dark on its shaded side.
- Settings now apply at once while in game: Character Shading, Mesh, Texture Replacements, Court Life, Original Mode and Button Icons change what's on screen straight away, mid-match too, instead of from the next match.

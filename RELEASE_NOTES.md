<!-- What's new since the last release, for players, in plain English: one "- " line per change. tools/release.sh puts this in the release and empties it. -->
- Settings → Display no longer has the Controller Vibration switch.
- Mods with a newer version on the store now show an Update button, and an Update all button on the Mods page and the main menu installs every update at once.
- Switching teams after everyone has picked their character now slides the cards across, as it does before.
- The sound words when the ball hits a player, the court, the net or a wall ("conk", "bumps", "rattle") no longer disappear behind see-through scenery.
- Mod character textures now wrap or stop at their edges as the model file says, so no thin seam shows down the middle of a face.
- The court flyover before a match no longer makes trees, props, scenery or players vanish or pop in as the camera sweeps past, on custom courts too.
- Custom courts can now have their own camera flyover before the match, so indoor courts no longer fly around outside their walls.
- In the mod store's Updates view, the Characters, Courts, Umpires and Texture packs tabs now count only the mods that have an update, not every mod in the store.
- Character select emotes now put the racket away for moves made without one, as they do in a match.
- In the browser on an iPhone held sideways, the picture now stays clear of the Dynamic Island, the notch and the home indicator instead of running under them.
- Online lobbies no longer ask you to download a custom character you already have when the other player's copy is a different version; your own copy is shown.
- Mod downloads and uploads now show their progress at the top of every menu screen, and when they finish the game offers to restart so the new mods load.
- Once your mod downloads and uploads have all finished, a Reload Mods button appears at the top of the menus, and if you put off reloading, picking Local or Online asks once more (Reload now or Play without them). Nothing asks you to reload while a download or upload is still going.
- The mod download and upload progress at the top of the menus now fills as mods download, counts every mod downloading at once, names the mod uploading now, and no longer moves your cursor on the screen you're on when the mod store list refreshes.
- Your Hugging Face sign-in for uploading mods is now saved where only you can read it, next to your settings (in the app's private storage on Android).

- The saved-match cards on the Continue screen now look like the rest of the menus.
- iPhone and Android: tapping the screen over and over while the game opens no longer leaves the on-screen controls dead. A button or stick stuck on a finger the phone lost track of frees itself the next time you touch it.
- iPhone and Android: the on-screen controls appear only after you tap the title screen to start, and that tap has to land away from the screen's edges and corners, so a finger holding the phone doesn't start the game.

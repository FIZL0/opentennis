- Saving a match works on iPhone and iPad.
- Online: quicker controls against far-away players, and opponents and the ball glide instead of jumping when the connection catches up.
- Online: new Netplay Delay setting (Settings → Online & Mods) to trade quicker controls for smoother opponents.
- Online: touch controls no longer make the other players jump around.
- Online: going out of sync shows a short note instead of covering the match.
- Tapping outside the pause menu no longer closes it.

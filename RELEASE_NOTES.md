<!-- What's new since the last release, for players, in plain English: one "- " line per change. tools/release.sh puts this in the release and empties it. -->
- Android: the start screen now looks like the main menu, with a Help page and a progress bar while the disc is copied.
- Desktop: the first-start disc picker is now your system's own file dialog (it also works on Windows and macOS now).
- The highlighted button on the main menu now gently pulses in and out.
- On the disc start screen, Escape, Backspace and K now go back from Help instead of opening the file picker.
- iPhone and iPad: if you installed OpenTennis with AltStore, SideStore or TrollStore, Update now opens that app to install the new version; otherwise it opens the release page as before.
- Custom characters that sit down on a seat after a game or in an emote line up with their seat again, instead of sitting turned off it.
- Online lobbies: your player's name tag now shows your online name until you pick a name (a default "Player" keeps 1P–4P).
- Online lobbies: while someone is still downloading, Start says Downloading… and the countdown only begins once everyone has finished; the wait (and the countdown) can be cancelled.
- iPhone and iPad: Settings → Online & Mods → Updates now shows what installed the game and how it was signed, to help us support more installers. It stays on your device.
- Computer players' second serves now use their usual strong toss as often as in the original, instead of always a soft one.
- Computer players no longer swing early on their serves: a well-timed computer serve now hits the sweet spot as often as in the original, so their topspin and slice serves come out more often.
- iPhone: with rotation lock on, the game now stays in landscape after you put the phone down and pick it up again, and starts in landscape next time too, instead of sometimes turning to portrait.

<!-- What's new since the last release, for players, in plain English: one "- " line per change. tools/release.sh puts this in the release and empties it. -->
- The on-screen keyboards (player names, mod search, your online name) now use a QWERTY layout.
- Phones and tablets: tapping a seat on the Controllers screen no longer adds a keyboard player that can't be removed.
- Phones and tablets: in a one-player game, pressing a button on a connected controller now switches to it straight away.
- With a safe area (notch, island or Safe Area margin), the fade after Game, Set, Match and the black screen when leaving a match now cover the whole screen.
- With a safe area, the menus' title tab now runs all the way to the screen's edge instead of stopping short.

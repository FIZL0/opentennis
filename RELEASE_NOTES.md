<!-- What's new since the last release, for players, in plain English: one "- " line per change. tools/release.sh puts this in the release and empties it. -->
- After the winners' celebration, the Game, Set, Match screen now cuts to a close view of the players and slowly circles them, as in the original, instead of freezing on the celebration's wide last shot.
- Android: players no longer go invisible or smear across the court on phones with older Snapdragon graphics (such as the Adreno 730 in the Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1).
- Settings → Graphics has a new Rendering option: Hardware (your graphics chip, fastest) or Software (drawn on the processor: much slower, but it looks the same everywhere), for when players, courts or effects look wrong or go missing. It asks first, then restarts the game. Software works on Windows, Linux and Steam Deck (the game brings its own software renderer there, updates included) and in browsers that offer one; phones and tablets are too slow to draw the game on the processor, so they stay on Hardware.

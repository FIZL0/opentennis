<!-- What's new since the last release, for players, in plain English: one "- " line per change. tools/release.sh puts this in the release and empties it. -->
- On-screen keyboards now use a QWERTY layout.
- Phones: tapping a player on the Controllers screen no longer adds a keyboard player you can't remove.
- Phones: pressing a button on a controller now switches to it straight away.
- With a safe area (phone notch or the Safe Area setting): the fade at the end of a match and the black screen when you leave now cover the whole screen.
- With a safe area: the menu's title tab now reaches the edge of the screen.
- iPhone: after a screenshot, a message tells you to tap the screen to get your controller working again.
- iPhone: the game now starts by itself once you add your disc image. No need to close and reopen it.
